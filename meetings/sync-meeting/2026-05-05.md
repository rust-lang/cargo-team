## (backlog) cargo should avoid releasing jobserver tokens when it's going to need a new one

https://github.com/rust-lang/cargo/issues/7786

Eric: Cargo drops jobserver tokens and then immediately grabs them again. And that can cause issues.

Josh: The comment being made here is "cargo races with the C compiler and rust build times are worse than C++ times" that sounds like you're trying to make Cargo cheat and do parts of its build faster. I'd be wary of that kind of heuristic mechanism.

Weihang: I remember that the jobserver crate has a ??. I don't know if that's worthy.

Josh: Yes. The jobserver protocol has many possibilities for potential stalls etc. Doing something more complicated by saying "I'm about to do something else, just hold the token" we'd get reports of people complaining that cargo is holding the token.

Josh: I think we should close this. If someone want to redesign the jobserver concept, it's a target rich environment.

Weihang: the Zig folks proposed this: https://github.com/rust-lang/cargo/issues/14102

Weihang: The other thing is we'll need to deal with the parallel frontend.

Josh: Rustc and cargo will steal tokens for what they're trying to do. We may need some heuristics to decide when we give threads to rustc from cargo but mainly, rustc and cargo should compete for the same tokens.

Arlo: The other thing missing is the ability to reserve memory vs. CPU

Eric: This could use more discussion. I'll try to follow up on this.
